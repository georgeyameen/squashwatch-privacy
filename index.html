SquashWatch Privacy Statement

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Hey there, Player!

I'm George Yameen, the creator of SquashWatch. I built this app because I love squash and wanted a better way to track my shots. Here's the straight talk on how your data is handled.

What SquashWatch Does & Doesn't Do

SquashWatch uses your Apple Watch sensors to detect your shots and keep your match score. Your shot data stays on your device.

Your Data Stays Put

SquashWatch processes your shot tracking locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your shot stats, match scores, performance metrics, and session history stay on your devices. Workout data (heart rate, calories) is saved to Apple Health only with your permission, and stays in your Apple Health account.

No Analytics

SquashWatch does not use third-party analytics services. No personal information or performance data is collected by us.

Kids Can Play Too

SquashWatch is safe for users of all ages. We don't collect any personal information that identifies users.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service:

  • RevenueCat (subscription management — processes anonymous purchase receipts, no personal data) - Privacy Policy

Changes to This Statement

If I ever update this privacy statement, I'll notify you through an app update and post the revised statement here.
